# 🏦 Alkansya

> **Hope begins with one small step.**

Alkansya helps you turn your family's dreams into a savings plan that fits your reality. Built on insights from over **163,000 Filipino families** from the **Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES) 2023**, it helps you see what's possible, take practical steps, and move closer to the future you're working hard to build.

Whether you're saving for your children's education, buying your first home, preparing for emergencies, starting a business, or simply building a more secure future, Alkansya is designed to help you make informed financial decisions with confidence.

---

## Why Alkansya?

Many Filipinos ask themselves:
- *"Normal ba ang gastos namin?"*
- *"Sapat ba ang naiipon namin?"*
- *"Kaya ba talaga naming maabot ang goal namin?"*

Unfortunately, there are very few tools that answer these questions using real Philippine household data.

Alkansya bridges that gap.

Instead of comparing you to unrealistic social media lifestyles or generic financial advice, Alkansya compares your situation with the spending and saving patterns of Filipino families living under similar circumstances.

Our goal isn't to judge your finances.

Our goal is to help you understand where you are today—and discover practical ways to move forward.

---

# What Alkansya Does

Based on your household information, Alkansya can help you:
- Estimate a realistic monthly savings target
- Understand whether your savings goal is achievable
- Compare your spending with similar Filipino families
- Identify spending categories with the greatest opportunity for improvement
- Estimate how long it may take to reach your goal
- Explore different savings scenarios
- Receive practical, data-informed recommendations tailored to your household

Every recommendation is intended to encourage progress—not perfection.

---

# Powered by Philippine Household Data

Alkansya is built using publicly available data from the:

**Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)**  
**Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES) 2023**

The underlying dataset includes information from more than **163,000 Filipino households** nationwide, covering:
- Household income
- Household expenditures
- Savings behavior
- Geographic location
- Household size
- Sources of income
- Demographic characteristics

The application uses statistical analysis and predictive machine learning models to generate personalized estimates based on patterns observed within the survey data.

# Important Disclaimer

Alkansya provides **educational and informational insights only.**

The recommendations generated by the application:
- are statistical estimates;
- are based on historical household survey data;
- do not guarantee future financial outcomes; and
- should not be interpreted as professional financial, legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.

Every family's financial situation is unique.

Unexpected events—including illness, unemployment, emergencies, inflation, disasters, or changes in income—may significantly affect actual financial outcomes.

Users should always exercise their own judgment and consult qualified professionals when making important financial decisions.

---

# About the Results

The insights generated by Alkansya are based on statistical models trained using PSA household survey data.

The application may use:
- household similarity matching
- predictive analytics
- machine learning
- statistical interpolation
- probability estimation

These methods identify patterns across large groups of households.

They **do not predict the future** and should not be interpreted as guarantees of financial success.

Instead, they provide a realistic picture of what has been observed among households with similar characteristics.

---

# Privacy

Alkansya is designed with privacy in mind.

The application does not require personally identifiable information such as:
- Name
- Address
- Government IDs
- Bank account information
- Credit card information

Calculations are based only on the financial information voluntarily entered by the user.

---

# Limitations

The PSA Family Income and Expenditure Survey is conducted periodically and represents household conditions during the survey period.

Accordingly:
- Economic conditions may have changed since the data were collected.
- Inflation, regional price changes, and policy reforms may affect present-day household finances.
- Household circumstances vary widely and cannot always be captured by statistical models.

The application should therefore be viewed as a financial planning aid rather than an exact prediction of future outcomes.
