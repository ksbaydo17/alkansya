About Alkansya · Tungkol dito

What this is. Alkansya is a financial mirror for Filipino families. It compares your household with 163,268 real families from the Philippine Statistics Authority's Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES) 2023 — the same survey behind the country's official poverty statistics — and shows what families like yours earn, spend, and manage to set aside.

Who made it. Alkansya was built by Karen Mae G. Sarinas-Baydo as a passion project and as part of the research behind Wala Tayong Pera, an upcoming book on financial stewardship for Filipino families.

How it works. A gradient boosting model trained on the FIES predicts what families with your income, household size, and location typically spend; cohort benchmarks show the range of savings rates families like yours actually sustain; and all peso figures are adjusted from 2023 survey prices to today's using the official CPI. Every assumption (interest, inflation) is visible and adjustable under Advanced.

What this is not. "Savings" here means income minus expenses in one survey year — not bank balances. Comparisons are associations from a survey, not guarantees about any one family, and nothing here is financial advice. This page stores and sends no data; what you type stays on your device.

Why it exists. Filipino families are often told they lack discipline with money. The data tells a different story: saving follows capacity. This tool exists so that families can see themselves honestly — walang hiya, walang panghuhusga — and find the one change most likely to help. Hope begins with one small step.

The survey behind this tool was taken in 2023; all peso benchmarks are adjusted to today's prices using a default +11% (PSA CPI: 3.2% in 2024, 1.7% in 2025, and a 2026 surge led by transport and food), and your income is converted to 2023 pesos before any comparison. Change the assumption under Advanced. *"Similar families spend" is a gradient boosting model prediction trained on PSA FIES 2023. "Savings" means income minus expenses in one survey year, not a bank balance. All percentages describe what similar households reported in that year; comparisons are associations, not guarantees, and none of this is financial advice. This page stores and sends no data.
